# Campaign pacer (Auto dial 1), number-day ledger, eligibility, never-reached pool, dispositions, caller ID

**Base:** `claude/campaign-pr07-callback-schedules` · **Migration:** `0118` · **Merge order:** 8 of 14

## Problem
This is the dialer itself:
- a loop that places campaign calls for free agents;
- one place that decides who may be dialed and how;
- the never-reached pool;
- dispositions that end or recycle leads;
- each campaign's own caller ID;
- a per-number, per-day ledger, so one number is not over-called across the companies of a call centre.

This replaces **#373** (the number-day ledger, renumbered 0118). #373 should be closed as superseded.

## Root cause
None of this existed.

## Fix
- **Migration 0118** adds `number_day_ledger` and `number_day_ledger_calls`.
- **Number ledger:**
  - the day is the lead's local day, never UTC;
  - the lock key is HMAC'd with a secret derived from `ACCESS_TOKEN_SECRET`;
  - the call-end write is a BullMQ job after commit;
  - it is off unless `NUMBER_LEDGER_ENABLED`.
- **BE-8 eligibility:** auto, preview only, or not now.
- **BE-9 pool:** never-reached people are called back by the company's schedule until they are reached or retired.
- **BE-10 pacer:**
  - it runs only on the leader;
  - it needs both `CAMPAIGN_DIALER_ENABLED` and `CAMPAIGN_PACER_ENABLED`;
  - it re-checks `FOR SHARE`, and re-checks the campaign's blockers inside the placing transaction;
  - stale reservations are released after 2 minutes;
  - one agent's error skips no one else;
  - mismatched numbers are retired after 5.
- **BE-6b dispositions:**
  - a disposition ends the lead, and "Not reached" recycles it;
  - a job repairs leads a call left calling;
  - the dispositions list adds `uncategorisedCount`.
- **BE-14 caller ID:** a campaign call shows its campaign's number or a local one.

## Why this is safe
- Nothing dials unless both env switches **and** the company switch are on, and the platform is not paused.
- The ledger writes nothing unless `NUMBER_LEDGER_ENABLED` (unset in production).
- Changes to the shared paths (`originate.ts`, `reconcile-call.ts`, `set-call-disposition.ts`, `notify.ts`) apply only to campaign calls or run behind the switches. Manual calls behave as before.
- Ships in **Deploy 2** (PR-02…PR-14 together, one restart). See the deploy plan.
