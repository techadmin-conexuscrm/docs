# Abandon-rate limit, wrap-up timer, live monitor, reach reports

**Base:** `claude/campaign-pr09-preview-lane` · **Migration:** `0120` · **Merge order:** 10 of 14

## Problem
- A predictive campaign must keep abandoned calls under 3%, and pause by itself if it can't.
- Supervisors need a live view of each campaign.
- Agents get a wrap-up countdown, with one alert when it runs out.
- Admins need reach rates.

## Root cause
None of this existed.

## Fix
- **Migration 0120** widens the `paused_reason` CHECK on `dialer_campaigns` for the automatic abandon pause.
- **BE-13:**
  - pacing odds and slowdown tiers;
  - an automatic pause at 3%;
  - the alert goes only to that company's supervisors, and is never broadcast when there are none.
- **BE-16a:**
  - the campaign's own wrap-up seconds;
  - one "overdue" alert per call, through a Redis NX key;
  - overdue wrap-ups are read oldest first, page by page.
- **BE-16b live monitor:**
  - available by GET, and pushed only to those who may read it;
  - rates cached 60 s.
- **BE-16c:** `GET /v1/dialer/reports/reach`.
