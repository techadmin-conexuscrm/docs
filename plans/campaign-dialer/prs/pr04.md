# Campaign dialing schema: leads, lists, attempts, sequences (BE-1)

**Base:** `claude/campaign-pr03-internal-do-not-call` · **Migration:** `0114` · **Merge order:** 4 of 14

## Problem
The dialer needs durable records:
- a lead per person and campaign;
- the lists they came from;
- every attempt;
- the retry sequences;
- each agent's per-campaign settings;
- each intake decision.

## Root cause
These tables did not exist.

## Fix
- **Migration 0114** adds `dialer_leads`, `dialer_lists`, `dialer_list_leads`, `dialer_list_imports`, `dialer_attempts`, `dialer_sequences`, `agent_campaign_settings` and `dialer_intake_decisions`.
  - They have composite `(campaign, organization)` foreign keys, so a row can never point at another tenant's campaign.
  - CHECKs cover the status and done-reason values.
- The Drizzle models are in `src/lib/db/models/dialer-leads.ts`.
- This PR is schema only. No code reads or writes the tables yet.

## Why this is safe
- The migration creates new, empty tables only.
- Its foreign keys to existing tables (`customers`, `orders`, `calls`, `users`, `organization_members`) are added while the new tables are empty.
- Ships in **Deploy 2** (PR-02…PR-14 together, one restart). See the deploy plan.
